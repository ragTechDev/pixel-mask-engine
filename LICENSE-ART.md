- Avatar masks: `src/avatar/masks/**`
- World masks (terrain tiles and object parts): `src/world/masks/**`
- Block layouts: `src/world/tilesets/**`
- Interface icons: `src/icons/**/*.mask.ts`
- Images rendered from the art: `docs/images/**`
